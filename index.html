Reel 02
The Ball

A small physics puzzle game — solo project, Unreal Engine.

GAMEPLAY

What it is

The Ball is a small, simple puzzle game built in Unreal Engine. The idea is exactly as it sounds — a ball has to get from the start of the level to the finish line. Nothing more complicated than that on paper, but the fun is in how it gets there.

Every level is really a physics problem: tilt, roll, and momentum are the only tools you have. Along the way there are ramps, gaps, narrow ledges, and moving pieces that punish careless rolling and reward a bit of patience. One wrong nudge and the ball falls off — no health bar, no penalty beyond starting the attempt over.

It's built to be picked up in seconds. No tutorial screens, no menus to dig through — you just start rolling, figure out the level by trying things, and know immediately when you've cleared it: the ball hits the finish line and the level's done.

Why I built it

This was as much about learning Unreal's physics and level-building workflow as it was about making something playable. Keeping the concept this small on purpose meant I could focus entirely on how the ball actually feels to control — the weight, the momentum, the exact point where a level goes from "just barely made it" to "fell off."

Reel 04
Photography

I shoot the way I look at things when no one's asking me to — quietly, and a little too long. A streetlamp caught in a flowering vine, birds lined up on a sandbank, the sun slipping behind a hill I walk past every evening. I'm not chasing the perfect frame; I'm chasing the moment right before I would've kept walking. Half of what I shoot is on my phone, on my way somewhere else. I like light more than subject, and stillness more than a good angle. Every photograph here is just me pausing for a second longer than I needed to.

Reel 06
Character Design

A class assignment: invent a character from a random topic and animal.

01 CHARACTER

Whaley Swift

This character came from a design assignment. Every student in the class was given a random topic and a random animal, and I got a whale who is a Taylor Swift fan.

So I put a Taylor Swift face on his hoodie and gave him a chain with a mic attached. Whaley Swift is a lovable, squishy whale and a very big fan.

Next to the full-body design, I drew an expression sheet to work out how he feels: angry, scared, sad, very happy, or just quietly listening to Taylor Swift.
